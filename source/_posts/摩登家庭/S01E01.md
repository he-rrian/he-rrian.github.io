# Part 1
## Phil, would you get them?
get sb. (把某人叫来/带来)

## Just a sec
一会儿、片刻

sec是second(秒)的缩写

## Why are you guys yelling at us when we're way upstairs? 
我们远在楼上你们喊也没用

yell at sb. (朝某人喊叫)

<span style="background-color:yellow;color:black;"><u>way作程度副词，表示远远地，大大地，加强语气</u></span>

    It's way too short. (太短了)

## Just text me
text sb. (给某人发短信)

## That's not gonna happen.
Gonna=going to，与be连用，表示未来，接下来

## Luke got his head stuck in the banister again.
banister：楼梯扶手、栏杆。

get stuck in (被困住，陷入困境)

get stuck in doing sth 也表示“积极做某事”

## come on
得了吧/加油

## I just don't want my kids to make the same bad mistakes I made. 
the same 后可接as或that

the same...as 同那一个相似/一样

the same...that 就是那一个

## vamos
(西班牙语) 加油

## He tripped him
他绊倒了他

## Let's take it down a notch.
冷静点

    notch   n. 等级；档次

## It's the number one (No. 1) village in all Colombia for all the Murders.
就所有的凶杀案而言，它是整个哥伦比亚中的数一数二村庄。

for 的意思是“就 …而言”。

## You gotta take that kid out.
gotta=have to

## He spent the first half with his hand in his pants!
他整个上半场光忙着抓裤裆了

first half (上半场)

spend time with (与xx共度时光)

## I've wanted to tell her off for the last six weeks. 
to tell someone off / to tell off sb. (生气，责备；呵斥；数落)

## She's adorable.
    adorable    adj. 可爱的

## precious
可爱的人

## you and your wife must be thrilled.
    thrilled    adj. 激动的

## We had initially asked one of our lesbian friends to be a surrogate.
    surrogate   n. 代孕

## Then we figured, they're already mean enough. 
### figure

n. 数字；人物；图形；价格；（人的）体形；画像

vi. 计算；出现；扮演角色

vt. 计算；认为；描绘；象征
### mean
v. 用意；意味着；意欲；引起；十分熟悉；严肃地说；意义重大；预示

adj. 吝啬的；刻薄的；低劣的；凶狠的；平均的；简陋的；出身卑贱的；（非正式）有效的

n. 平均值；中庸

## Ick
下头

##  Everybody fawning over Lily.
fawn over (奉承，拍马屁)

## Look at that baby with those cream puffs.
cream puffs (泡芙，娘娘腔)

## Note to all of you who judge.
note to sb. (请注意，特此告知)

## Love knows no race, creed or gender.
    creed   信条，信仰

## And shame on you, you small-minded, ignorant
### shame on you
责备你，对你感到羞耻(用于表达对某人的不道德行为或错误感到失望或愤怒)

**shame for you** 为你感到羞耻(用于表达对某人感到羞耻或尴尬)
### small-minded
### ignorant
adj. 无知的

## We would like to pay for everyone's headsets.
    headset n.头戴式耳机
